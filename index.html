Privacy Policy for TaskRemind AI

Last Updated: July 28, 2026

TaskRemind AI ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication credentials when you create an account.
  • Reminder and Task Data: Titles, descriptions, categories, due dates, and completion records.
  • Photos: Optional photos you attach to reminders or tasks.
  • Notes: Text notes you add to your reminders and tasks.
  • Collaboration Data: Project sharing information, collaborator email addresses, and shared project permissions.
  • AI Input Data: Text prompts and voice recordings sent to AI features for task generation and assistance.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, app version.
  • Usage Data: Feature usage frequency, screen navigation patterns, task completion rates, and streak data.
  • Purchase Data: Subscription status and transaction information (processed by RevenueCat).
  • Local Storage: Data stored locally on your device for offline functionality.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and maintain the reminder and task management features, sync your data across devices, send push notifications, enable collaboration, process AI-powered features, manage subscriptions, improve the app, and provide customer support.

Data Storage and Security

Most data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and is not accessible to us unless you choose to sync. If you create an account, data is synced to Supabase servers and encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information, share it with advertisers, or use it for marketing without consent. We may share data only with service providers (Supabase, RevenueCat, Expo, AI providers), collaborators you explicitly invite, or when required by law.

Collaboration Data Sharing

When you add collaborators to a project, they can view and edit project tasks, reminders, and notes. Your email address is visible to collaborators you share with. You can remove collaborators or delete a shared project at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data. You can export your data, delete your account from the Settings screen, or contact us for assistance.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Camera and Microphone Access

The app may request camera access for attaching photos and microphone access for voice-to-text AI features. These permissions are optional and only used when you explicitly choose them.

AI Features and Data Processing

AI features process text prompts and voice recordings converted to text. AI providers may temporarily store data for processing but do not use it to train models or for other purposes. You choose when to use AI features.

Notifications

We send local push notifications for your reminders. You can control notification permissions and preferences in your device settings and within the app.

Data Deletion Without Account Deletion

You can delete some or all of your data without deleting your account. In the TaskRemind AI app, you can delete any individual reminder, task, todo, project, template, category, note, or attachment at any time. Deleted items are removed from your synced account and, once synced, from our servers.

Alternatively, you can request deletion of specific data by emailing us at taskremindai@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and listing the data you want removed. We will process your request within 30 days.

Data Deleted

  • Reminders, tasks, todos, and projects you choose to delete
  • Notes, photos, and attachments associated with deleted items
  • Saved templates and custom categories you choose to delete
  • Completed history entries you choose to delete

Data Kept

  • Your account profile and email address (unless you also request account deletion)
  • Remaining data you have not chosen to delete
  • Transaction records retained by RevenueCat for legal and accounting purposes

Retention Period

Items deleted in the app are removed immediately from active storage. Backups may retain deleted data for up to 30 days, after which it is permanently removed.

Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. To start the process, open the TaskRemind AI app, go to Settings > Account > Delete Account, and confirm the deletion. You will be signed out and all data associated with your account will be removed from our servers.

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by emailing us at taskremindai@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." We will process your request within 30 days.

Data Deleted

  • Your account profile and email address
  • All reminders, tasks, todos, and projects you created
  • All notes, photos, and attachments you uploaded
  • All collaboration and sharing data you initiated
  • Your notification preferences and app settings
  • Your subscription record (billing details are retained by RevenueCat for tax and accounting purposes)

Data Kept

  • Anonymous analytics and diagnostic data that cannot identify you
  • Transaction records retained by RevenueCat for legal and accounting purposes

Retention Period

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Backups may retain deleted data for up to 30 days after deletion, after which it is permanently removed.

Data Retention

Active account data is retained while your account is active. Deleted accounts are permanently removed within 30 days. Local data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear data.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and cloud data storage
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase processing
  • Expo — app infrastructure
  • AI Service Providers — AI assistant and task generation

International Data Transfers

Data may be transferred to and processed in countries where our servers are located. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted in the app, the "Last Updated" date will be updated, and significant changes may be announced with an in-app notification.
